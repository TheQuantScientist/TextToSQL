# 📊 Global Finance, Development & Well-being Datasets

This repository contains curated datasets from **Kaggle** covering finance, income, development, and happiness indicators across the world. These datasets provide valuable insights into global economic trends, well-being, and development over time.  

## 📂 Data Sources

### 1. Finance & Economics  
- **Dataset**: [Finance and Economics Dataset (2000–Present)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/khushikyad001/finance-and-economics-dataset-2000-present)  
- **Description**: Contains key financial and economic indicators spanning multiple countries and years, enabling analysis of macroeconomic performance, trade, and fiscal policy.  

---

### 2. Global Income Statistics  
- **Dataset**: [Global Income Statistics](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/konradb/global-income-statistics)  
- **Description**: Provides worldwide income distribution data, useful for studying inequality, median income levels, and cross-country comparisons of wealth.  

---

### 3. World Happiness Report  
- **Dataset**: [World Happiness Report (2005–Present)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/usamabuttar/world-happiness-report-2005-present)  
- **Description**: Tracks global well-being through survey-based happiness scores, alongside socio-economic and psychological factors such as GDP, social support, life expectancy, and perceptions of corruption.  

---

### 4. Global Development Indicators  
- **Dataset**: [Global Development Indicators (2000–2020)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/michaelmatta0/global-development-indicators-2000-2020)  
- **Description**: A comprehensive dataset covering education, health, infrastructure, and demographic metrics, allowing in-depth analysis of international development trends.
